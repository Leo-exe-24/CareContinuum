ABOUT CARECONTINUUM

Connecting fragmented care with explainable AI.

CareContinuum is a healthcare hackathon and research prototype for exploring continuity-of-care patterns across longitudinal visit records.

METHOD

Bice–Boxerman continuity

For provider visit counts nᵢ and total valid provider visits N, COC = ((Σ nᵢ²) − N) / (N × (N − 1)). Fewer than two valid provider-linked visits yields not calculable. UPC measures the largest provider share; SECON measures same-provider consecutive visits.

MODEL

XGBoost4J and TreeSHAP

A binary XGBoost model operates on patient-level aggregates. The patient-level split prevents the same patient from crossing train and hold-out sets. TreeSHAP contributions are model explanations, not causal or clinical evidence.

DEMO DATA

Synthetic assumptions

Bundled records are generated for software demonstration. Correlations between utilization, fragmented care, access factors, and target outcome are invented assumptions; they are not claims about real-world clinical relationships.

LIMITATIONS & ETHICS

Research and decision support only

  • Not a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, or autonomous decision system.
  • No clinical validation, prospective evaluation, or effectiveness claim is made.
  • Real data can contain bias, missingness, leakage, and distribution shift.
  • COC does not capture every dimension of continuity.
  • Use only de-identified, appropriately licensed data in this prototype.
